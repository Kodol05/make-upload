# Seedance 시작 프레임 생성 프롬프트

## 공통 규칙

각 이미지는 Seedance 1.5 Pro 이미지투비디오의 첫 프레임으로 직접 사용한다. 시작 상태만 정지해 보여 주며, 동작 중간 자세와 모션 블러를 만들지 않는다.

```text
Friendly non-childish stylized 3D public-information animation for international university students in Korea. Match the attached character and environment references exactly. Clean rounded forms, believable everyday material textures, warm morning daylight, soft global illumination, gentle contact shadows, warm ivory, pale wood, deep teal and clean blue accents, calm practical composition. One clear pre-action moment, stable anatomy, no motion blur. No readable text, letters, numbers, labels, symbols, recycling icons, logos, brands, watermark, flags, school emblem, extra people, duplicate objects, malformed hands, unsafe handling, broken objects. 16:9 landscape, high resolution.
```
